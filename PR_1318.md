# PR: Trigger on-chain Stellar clawback before marking expired-deal investments REFUNDED

Closes #1318

## Summary

`TradeDealsCronService.refundPendingInvestmentsOnExpiredDeals()` used to flip every
`PENDING` investment on an `expired` deal straight to `REFUNDED` and send an email.
No Stellar transaction was ever submitted, so investors saw "Refunded" while their
funds never moved on-chain.

This change makes the refund a two-stage, verifiable flow:

- The cron now emits a typed `stellar.clawback` queue job for **each** matched
  investment **before** touching its status.
- Only investments whose clawback job was successfully queued are moved to
  `REFUNDED`. If queueing fails for an investment, it stays `PENDING` and is
  retried on the next hourly run; no "refunded" email is sent for it.
- A new `StellarClawbackConsumer` actually performs the on-chain operation:
  it reloads the investment with its deal/investor, decrypts the issuer secret
  and calls `StellarService.clawbackTokens()`. Only after that succeeds does it
  write the final `REFUNDED` status and mark its idempotency key done.
- The consumer uses the existing idempotency lease protocol, requeues transient
  failures with backoff, and on permanent failure puts the investment back into
  `PENDING` and emits an `admin.alert` — so a refunded-looking DB row can never
  silently hide a missing on-chain transaction.
